# MyGigMint – Project Overview

## Project Information

**Project Name:** MyGigMint

**Project Type:** AI-Powered Micro Job Marketplace

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Prepared By:** MyGigMint Product Team

---

# Executive Summary

MyGigMint is a next-generation AI-powered micro job marketplace that connects businesses needing small digital tasks completed with users seeking flexible online earning opportunities.

Unlike traditional micro job websites, MyGigMint focuses on a modern user experience, intelligent job recommendations, transparent payments, fraud prevention, and scalable architecture.

The platform is designed to support both Bangladesh and international markets.

---

# Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted AI-powered micro job platforms where anyone can earn money online safely, quickly, and transparently.

---

# Mission

Build a secure, scalable, and user-friendly ecosystem that allows people to:

* Earn through simple online tasks
* Hire workers easily
* Receive fast payments
* Grow through referrals
* Increase productivity using AI-powered tools

---

# Objectives

The first production version (MVP) should allow users to:

* Create an account
* Verify email
* Complete profile
* Browse available jobs
* Submit completed work
* Receive payment after approval
* Withdraw earnings
* Invite friends
* Purchase premium memberships

Employers should be able to:

* Create jobs
* Manage budgets
* Review submissions
* Approve or reject work
* Track campaign performance

Administrators should be able to:

* Manage users
* Manage jobs
* Moderate submissions
* Process withdrawals
* Configure referral commissions
* Manage premium plans
* View analytics
* Control platform settings
