# Chapter 25 – Admin, Moderation & Platform Management System

# Part 1 – Sections 1–35

## 1. Admin System Overview
The platform shall provide a centralized administrative system for managing users, content, transactions, security, operations, configuration, and platform governance.

## 2. Admin Architecture
The admin system shall be separated from the public user interface and protected by strong authentication and authorization.

## 3. Admin Dashboard
The dashboard shall provide authorized administrators with an overview of important platform activities.

## 4. Admin Roles
The platform shall support specialized administrative roles according to operational requirements.

## 5. Super Administrator
The Super Administrator shall have the highest level of authorized platform management privileges.

## 6. Operations Administrator
The Operations Administrator shall manage day-to-day platform operations.

## 7. Support Administrator
The Support Administrator shall manage support-related activities.

## 8. Finance Administrator
The Finance Administrator shall manage authorized financial operations.

## 9. Security Administrator
The Security Administrator shall manage security events, risk controls, and security investigations.

## 10. Moderation Administrator
The Moderation Administrator shall manage user-generated content and platform moderation.

## 11. Admin Authentication
All administrative accounts shall use secure authentication mechanisms.

## 12. Admin MFA
Multi-factor authentication shall be required for privileged administrative accounts.

## 13. Admin Session Security
Administrative sessions shall use stricter expiration and security controls.

## 14. Admin Authorization
Every administrative operation shall be authorized server-side.

## 15. Admin Permissions
Permissions shall follow the principle of least privilege.

## 16. Permission Groups
Permissions may be grouped by:
- Users
- Finance
- Jobs
- Marketplace
- Security
- Support
- Configuration

## 17. Permission Review
Administrative permissions shall be reviewed periodically.

## 18. Privileged Actions
High-risk administrative actions shall require appropriate privileges.

## 19. Admin Activity Logging
Administrative actions shall be logged.

## 20. Admin Audit Trail
Audit records shall include:
- Administrator
- Action
- Target
- Timestamp
- Result

## 21. Admin Search
Administrators shall be able to search authorized platform records.

## 22. Admin Filtering
Administrative lists shall support configurable filtering.

## 23. Admin Sorting
Records shall support sorting by relevant fields.

## 24. Pagination
Large administrative datasets shall use pagination or equivalent mechanisms.

## 25. Bulk Operations
Authorized administrators may perform approved bulk actions.

## 26. Bulk Operation Safety
High-risk bulk operations shall require confirmation and appropriate authorization.

## 27. Bulk Operation Logging
Bulk actions shall be recorded in audit logs.

## 28. Admin Notifications
Administrators shall receive important operational and security notifications.

## 29. Admin Alerts
Critical platform events shall generate administrative alerts.

## 30. Admin Dashboard Metrics
The dashboard may display:
- Active Users
- Transactions
- Jobs
- Orders
- Withdrawals
- Security Events

## 31. Admin Quick Actions
Authorized administrators may access commonly used management actions.

## 32. Admin Dashboard Customization
Dashboard widgets may be customized according to role.

## 33. Admin Access Restrictions
Administrative access may be restricted by security policies.

## 34. Emergency Admin Controls
Authorized administrators may activate emergency platform controls.

## 35. Part 1 Completion Standard
Part 1 shall be complete when the administrative architecture, roles, authentication, MFA, authorization, dashboard, permissions, audit logging, alerts, search, filtering, bulk operations, and emergency controls are implemented.
